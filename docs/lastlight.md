The sea has swallowed every shore but one. Your skiff is small; the lighthouse is stubborn. Keep both alive through five tides and defeat the Leviathan.

## Start playing

Extract the ZIP and double-click **`PLAY LASTLIGHT.bat`**. Click **Light the lantern**. Choose **Navigator** on the title screen for a gentler first run, or leave **Mariner** selected for the original challenge.

| Control | Action |
|:--|:--|
| WASD / arrows | Steer |
| Space | Dash; briefly avoid damage and hurt creatures you pass through |
| E | Shockwave; damage nearby enemies and clear nearby hostile shots |
| Hold left mouse | Aim manually toward the pointer |
| Release left mouse | Auto-fire at the nearest enemy in range |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Choose an intermission upgrade |
| P / Escape | Pause or resume |
| M | Mute / unmute all sound |
| F | Toggle fullscreen |

## How a run works

1. Stay alive and keep enemies off the central lighthouse.
2. Collect **gold wreckage** to repair the lantern and **green salvage** to heal your hull.
3. After each of the first four tides, clear remaining enemies to dock. Both health pools receive repairs; choose one permanent upgrade.
4. Survive the fifth tide's spawning period to summon the **Leviathan**. Destroy it to see dawn.

The tide spawning windows are 35, 38, 41, 44, and 47 seconds. Enemy clearing, upgrade choices, and the boss add to that time. There is no verified average completion time.

## A few useful instincts

- Watch the lantern as well as your hull. Most slow creatures head toward the center; hunters chase you.
- Don't hoard the shockwave forever. It recharges, and it can open a safe pickup route.
- Dash through a pack when its offensive damage is worth the risk of where you land.
- Extra projectiles help cover space. Rate, damage, and piercing improve sustained pressure. Hull or salvage regeneration can make a rough run recoverable.

Best scores save in browser storage when available. Saved scores are local to your browser; there is no server, account, or resume-from-mid-run save.
