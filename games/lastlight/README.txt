LASTLIGHT - a complete offline arcade game
=========================================
Double-click PLAY LASTLIGHT.bat. No installation, server, assets, or internet needed.
Requires a modern desktop browser (Edge, Chrome, or Firefox).

WASD / Arrow keys: steer
Space: dash (invulnerable; also damages enemies)
E: shockwave (clears hostile projectiles)
Hold left mouse button: aim manually toward the cursor
Release mouse: automatically target the nearest enemy
P / Escape: pause
M: mute
F: fullscreen
1 / 2 / 3: choose an upgrade between waves

Protect both your skiff and the central lighthouse. Collect gold wreckage to
restore the lighthouse; green salvage heals your skiff. Each cleared tide
repairs both. Five tides lead to the final Leviathan battle. There is a complete
victory ending, defeat screen, restart, and locally saved best score.

Navigator mode offers more health, stronger repairs, and gentler incoming damage.
You can select it on the title screen. Audio starts when you begin playing.
The entire game is procedural, including its art, sound effects, and music.
Your browser may restrict saved scores for local files; play still works.
