ONE PROMPT, TWO WORLDS
======================
Two complete games made for the same open-ended generation challenge.

On Windows: extract the entire ZIP, then double-click PLAY BOTH.bat.
Select 1 for LASTLIGHT or 2 for Last Light Lancer.

On other desktop systems: open index.html inside either game's folder.
No installation, assets, accounts, API keys, or internet connection needed.
A keyboard, mouse, and modern browser are required.

LASTLIGHT / GPT-6 Astra / medium reasoning
Protect the lighthouse through five tides, then defeat the Leviathan.
WASD: steer. Space: dash. E: shockwave. Weapons auto-fire.
Hold left mouse to aim manually. P: pause. M: mute. F: fullscreen.
Navigator mode on the title screen gives a gentler first run.

LAST LIGHT LANCER / GPT-5.6 Sol / high reasoning
Recover six signal cores, then hold the starting extraction ring for 10 seconds.
WASD: move. Mouse: aim. Hold left mouse: fire. Space: dash.
Hold E near a core. 1/2/3: buy cannon/engine/shield upgrades.
P: pause. M: music. T: audio test.

Model settings are attributed by the challenge owner. The games are preserved
as supplied for this comparison. The exhibit is an independent case study,
not an official OpenAI benchmark or a controlled model ranking.
